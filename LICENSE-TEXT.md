# Licence

This repository holds two kinds of work, and each has the licence that suits it.

| What | Licence |
|---|---|
| **Code**: every engine and script (`.py`, `.sh`) | **MIT** — the text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE) |
| **Texts**: the paper, the reports, the audits, the readings, the logs and every other document | **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** — [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) |

The paper is also published on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23188298.

## What CC BY 4.0 allows, and what it asks

You may copy, redistribute, translate, adapt and build upon these texts, for any purpose, including commercially. In return you must:

1. **give appropriate credit** — name the author, **Rafael Amichis Luengo** (cited as *Amichis Luengo, R.*), and cite the work as in [CITATION.md](CITATION.md);
2. **link to the licence**;
3. **say whether you changed anything**, and not suggest that the author endorses your use.

## What MIT allows, and what it asks

You may use, copy, modify, merge, publish and distribute the code freely, provided the copyright notice and the permission notice in [LICENSE](LICENSE) are kept in every copy or substantial portion of it.

## Scholarly use

A licence governs copying; scholarly practice governs credit, and it asks for more. If a result, an idea, a counterexample or a number from this repository is used in research — whether or not any text or code is copied — please cite the source: the paper for everything it contains, and the specific document of the record otherwise.

## What is not covered

- The papers of other authors that were read are **not** in this repository; they are only cited.
- One line of one document of the record was redacted because it named a private file of the author; it is marked where it occurs ([record/README.md](record/README.md)).
- The status of each document is the one it states. The texts are provided **as is**, without warranty that they are free of errors; several of the working documents were later corrected, and the record says so. The authoritative mathematical statements are those of the paper.
