# PDF defects found by cold reader 6 (working list; the summary goes to REPORT §3–§5)

## Global defects (GD)
- GD1 — straight ASCII apostrophes: `pdftotext` finds 43 U+0027 and no U+2019 (logs/apos.log): «Bai's», «Gao et al.'s», «constructors' reports», «Kostant's», … TeX sets ’. Fix: map `'` → `’` in running text (never inside code or primes).
- GD2 — the number sets are set in math italic: `Z`, `Z_2`, `Z_{≥0}`, `Q`, `Q_2`, `F_2` (p. 2 onwards). A TeX user expects ℤ, ℤ₂, ℚ₂, 𝔽₂ (`\mathbb`). The italic field `F_2` also collides with the operator `F` of the hyperalgebra (`F^{(m)}`, `F_2^n`). Fix: map these names to double-struck letters in the builder.
- GD3 — accents over italic letters are misplaced: the macron of `K̄` sits on the left stem of the italic K instead of centred over the letter (skewed right, as TeX's `\bar`) — p. 2 (table, Theorem DW row, 400 dpi crop scratch/p200/p02_kbar). (Others checked below.)
- GD4 — every table is fully ruled (vertical rules between all columns, a rule under every row, shaded header). A top journal sets tables with horizontal rules only (booktabs). Low weight.
- GD5 — hyphenation inside a word that already contains a hyphen: «non-in-/creasing» (p. 1, abstract, line 7). TeX breaks such a compound only at its hyphen. (Systematic search in §5.)
- GD6 — digits and formulas inside bold sentences are set light: «the whole **2**-part» shows a light «2» before a bold «-part» (p. 1, abstract lines 3 and 13). Low weight; TeX would do the same with `$2$`, but the author can write the digit as text in a bold phrase.
- GD7 — statements are set in grey boxes with a dark left bar (the md's block quotes). A top journal sets theorems in italic or with a bold run-in head, without shading. Low weight; a matter of house style.
- GD8 — sentences that begin with a formula: «`n ≥ 1`, `K(Q_n)` is the sandpile group …» (§1.0, p. 2, first line); «`a_n` is the number of factors …» (§1.3, p. 5). Knuth's and Halmos's rule (and every copy editor's): never start a sentence with a symbol. Fix: «For `n ≥ 1`, …»; «The number `a_n` is …».
- GD9 — commas inside subscripts and limits get no space: «1≤λ≤n,λ≡n (mod 2)» (p. 4), «j even,2≤j≤n» (p. 5, Corollary 1.4). (TeX also drops the space there, which is why authors write `\,` or `\substack`.) Fix: thin space after a comma in scripts, or stack the conditions.
- GD10 — ellipsis on the baseline between relations or operators: «`c_1(Q_n) ≥ c_2(Q_n) ≥ …`» (md line 116, p. 5) and «`… + 2^{h+1} − 1`» twice (md line 410, proof of Theorem 4.4). TeX style: `\cdots` (⋯) between relations and binary operators, `\ldots` between commas; the paper itself writes `c_2 = ⋯ = c_{n+1}` in Corollary 1.6. Fix: «≥ ⋯», «⋯ + 2^{h+1} − 1».
- GD11 — bold statements with formulas: since v4 sets formulas light, a bold span of the md that mixes words and formulas prints its words bold and its formulas light. `scratch/boldmix.py` (logs/boldmix.log) finds 26 such spans. In headings («The mod-2 layer.», «Proposition 9.3 (shift 1).») this is what TeX gives without `\boldmath`; in emphasized statements it leaves stray bold words: Corollary 1.6 «`c_n = …` **for** `n ≥ 3`», «`c_{n+1} = g(n − 1)` **for** `n ≥ 4`» (md 125–126, p. 6); Theorem 4.4 «`M_{DD'} = …` **if** `D' ⊆ D`» (md 403); md 914 «**with** … **entrywise**»; md 990 and 996 «**at every level**»; md 935 «**is a ring homomorphism**, … **for every** …, **and**». Fix: drop the bold where the span is mostly formula, or set the whole span bold (bold math) consistently.
- GD12 — the end-of-proof mark: a tall filled bar «∎» set one space after the last word (e.g. p. 10, proof of Lemma 2.1: «= 2s_j. ∎»), never flush right. In TeX (amsthm) the mark is pushed to the right margin of the last line (`\qed`, `\hfill`), usually an open box □. Fix: right-align the mark (CSS `float: right` inside the last line box, or a flexible space before it).
- GD13 — hyphenation that leaves two letters on the next line: «reducibili-/ty» (p. 13, proof of Proposition 3.1(d)). TeX's English setting (`\righthyphenmin = 3`) never does this. (Systematic count in §5 of the report.)
- GD14 — the headings «2. … of SL₂» and «3.2 Lifting from F₂» use Unicode subscript digits in upright bold, while the same symbols are set as mathematics everywhere else (`SL_2` upright with a stacked 2; `F_2` italic). Fix: set heading mathematics with the math renderer (bold).
- GD3 (upgraded, p. 14) — the misplaced overbar is not cosmetic: in the proof of Lemma 3.5, md line 324 «(`N̄ := N ⊗ F_2`)» prints with a bar so short and so far left that it merges with the left serif of the italic N; at reading size the line says «(N := N ⊗ F_2)», and the display above it «V^{⊗3} ⊗ N^{[1]} ≅ (V ⊗ V^{[1]} ⊗ N^{[1]}) ⊕ …» loses the distinction between `N` and `N̄` (600 dpi crop scratch/p200/p14_nbar600b-14.png). The same holds for every `K̄`, and must be checked for `X̄`, `ā`, `χ̄`, `ω̄`, `b̄`. Fix: build the bar as a positioned accent over the letter's optical centre (with the italic skew), as `\bar` in TeX, not as the combining macron U+0304 that the font does not position over math italic letters.
- GD15 — a condition after a displayed formula is set at one word space: §4.1 (p. 15) «… + Σ_{s>s'} K_{ss'}(D) x ι_s (x ∈ N),» reads as `ι_s` applied to `(x ∈ N)`. TeX style: `\quad (x ∈ N)`. Fix: a quad (1 em) before a parenthetical condition in a display (the md has two spaces there).
- GD16 — delimiters not sized to their content in displays: Proposition 4.1 (p. 15), «K'_{us'}(f) = −½[π_{s'}(2f + 1)K_{us'}(2f) + … + Σ_{s'<s<u} K_{ss'}(2f + 1)K_{us}(2f)]» — text-size brackets around a display-size `Σ` with its limits under. TeX: `\Bigl[ … \Bigr]`. Fix: size the brackets to the operator (or set the sum in text style there).
- GD17 — the vulgar fraction «½» (U+00BD) in formulas (md lines 366, 410 ×3, 867, 998): in a display it prints as a small text fraction. TeX: `\frac12` in displays, `\tfrac12` or `1/2` in text. Low weight.
- GD3 (extent, logs/accents.log) — every letter accented with a combining mark is affected: `K̄` 19, `X̄` 14, `N̄` 6, `d̂` 6, `χ̄` 4, `χ̃` 3, `L̄` 3, `f̄` 1, `b̄` 1, `ω̄` 1 (58 in all). The precomposed `ŷ` (U+0177) and `ĉ` (U+0109) are set right. On p. 21 the hat of `d̂_r` sits on the ascender of the italic d and the symbol reads «đ_r» (300 dpi crop scratch/p200/p21_a-21.png).
- GD18 — a citation key hyphenated across lines: «[ABER-» / «S55]» (p. 22, §7.1, lines 4–5). Keys, like file names, must never be broken. Fix: no-break span around every citation key. (pdftotext rejoins it, so a text check does not see it; systematic search on the bbox in §5.)
- GD19 — a binary operator set without its spaces: «‖z − y‖^2+2Σ_j» (p. 22, proof of Lemma 7.1; md line 559 has «‖z − y‖^2 + 2Σ_j»). The renderer took this «+» for a unary sign after the closing «‖». Fix: treat «‖» … «‖» as a pair (the closing bar is an Ord, as in TeX), so the following «+» is binary.
- GD20 — a display line that mixes formulas and words sets every formula as a separate block with a quad on each side, even a one-letter variable inside the words: Lemma 7.3 (p. 23) «fit(x) = [max(Z_1, 0), min(Z_2, 0)]  and the integer fit of  x  is  [max(X_1, 0), min(X_2, 0)],». TeX: one display, `\quad\text{and the integer fit of } x \text{ is}\quad`. Fix: a quad only between a formula and the words, and ordinary word spaces around an inline variable.
- GD21 — words inside scripts lose their spaces: «Σ_{lastL positions ofP} x» (p. 23, proof of Lemma 7.3; md «Σ_{last L positions of P}»); also «minΔ» in the limits of §5 (p. 19). Fix: keep word spaces in `\text{…}` inside scripts, and a thin space after an operator name.
- GD22 — lists whose items carry their own labels are also bulleted: «• (U1) …», «• (U2) …» (Theorem 7.4, p. 24), «• (E) …», «• (M) …», «• (G) …», «• (Cut) …», «• (Λ) …» (Theorem 7.8, p. 25). A journal sets the label itself as the hanging list marker. Fix: render «- (X) …» items as a labelled list without bullets.
- GD23 — `max` and `min` in displays carry their limits at the side, not under: «c_3 := v'(f) = max_{F_1} v', c_4 := v'(I ∖ f) = min_{F_2} v'» (p. 26, step 7 of the proof of Theorem 7.8, md 656); the other display with such a limit is md 725 (Proposition 9.3). In TeX's display style the limits of `\max`, `\min` go under, like those of `Σ`. Fix: treat operator names with limits as large operators in displays.
- GD23 (more) — also «μ = φ(Y_l) + mean_{D⊆P_l}[…]» (p. 30, proof of Proposition 9.6, md 747): a display operator name with its limit at the side.
- GD24 — a short inline formula broken across a page: «Also, if n ≡» (last line of p. 30) / «3 (mod 4), then …» (first line of p. 31), proof of Corollary 1.6, item 2. Fix: no break inside `n ≡ 3 (mod 4)`.
- GD25 — an n-ary direct sum set as the binary sign: «Z_2 ⊕ Syl_2 K̄(n) ≅ ⊕(2X)^{m_λ} = 2(Z_2 ⊕ Syl_2 K(Q_n)) ≅ …» (p. 32, proof of Proposition 10.3, md 803 — the display created in v4 by H18). The renderer makes `⊕` a large operator only when it carries a script; here the sum over the families has no index and prints at binary size. TeX: `\bigoplus_λ (2X)^{m_λ}`. Fix: «⊕_λ (2X)^{m_λ(n)}» in the md (it is the only such place: logs/grep_bigop.log).
- GD26 — 2 × 2 matrices are written as nested lists, «[[X, 0], [(Y − X)/2, Y]]», and printed so: 12 places (md 820, 826, 827, 837, 851, 854, 857, 861, 873, 883, 998, 1027; logs/grep_mat.log), among them the definition of `glue` (p. 33), the twist map of §10.3, the pivot block `[[P, C], [B, Dd]]` and `glue(W_−, W_+)` in Theorem 10.22. A TeX user sees programming notation; a journal sets `\begin{pmatrix} X & 0 \\ (Y−X)/2 & Y \end{pmatrix}` (inline: `\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}…\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)`). Likewise the words «column 1 += column 2, row 2 −= row 1» (md 837, p. 33) are code. Fix: matrices in the builder; «add column 2 to column 1 and subtract row 1 from row 2».
- GD27 — a solidus before a display-size product: «(−1)^s(L!/∏ m_i!) · ∏_i ζ_{m_i}(f_i) / ∏_{j=0}^{s} π(f_j)», «y^Σ_L(f) = (−1)^L L! η^L / ∏_{d=f−L}^{f} π(d)», «(𝔡_1)_D = … (2η)^{o_D} / ∏_{d<o_D} π(d)» (p. 36). In a display TeX sets these as `\frac{…}{\prod …}`; a «/» followed by a full-size `∏` with limits under is not TeX's style. Low weight.
- GD28 — the relation «=:» is split into two relations with a space between them: «min Δ = : δ(Δ)» (Lemma 4.5, p. 18, md 417) and «Θ(B) = Θ(C) = : β» (proof of Theorem 10.15, p. 38, md 961); `pdftotext` reads «= :» in both. The paper writes «:=» right everywhere else. TeX: `=:` (or `\eqqcolon`) is one relation. Fix: tokenize «=:» as one relation, like «:=».
- GD29 — in the narrow columns of the §12 tables a number is separated from its noun at a line end: «48 006» / «house-checks, 0» / «failures» (p. 43, row of Theorem 7.8), «… · 301» / «entries» (row of Propositions 5.2–5.4), «8 001» / «boundaries, 192 024» / «non-zero shifts» (row of strict (Cut)), and a number alone on the last line of a cell («predicts a wrong Smith form in» / «262»; «… in 18 606 of» / «192 024»). Version 4 says (CORRECTIONS §2, rows 11, 17) that rules (29), (30), (32) keep a number with its word; they do not act in table cells. Fix: no-break space between a number and the next word, also in tables.
- GD30 — the reference list: labels run into the entries and continuation lines are indented less than the longest label (e.g. «[ABERS55] M. Ayer, …» / «sampling with …», p. 48); a journal sets the labels in a column of their own (amsalpha). arXiv numbers are given for [CSX17], [DHS18], [GMMY24], [IKKY23], [Yue24] but not for [DJ14] (arXiv:1308.2335), [RT14] (arXiv:1301.2977) or [LZ22] (arXiv:2012.15235), although those preprints are in the sources: inconsistent. [Kos66] «Groups over Z» (the original title has ℤ; GD2). Low weight.
- GD12 (cause) — the mark «∎» (U+220E) is not in STIX Two Text and Chrome draws it from the fallback font STIXGeneral-Regular (STIX 1.x): `pdffonts` per page (logs/pdffonts_pages.log) shows STIXGeneral-Regular on exactly the 30 pages that carry a «∎» (logs/qed_pages.log: p. 10–25, 27–33, 35–41). That is why the mark is a tall narrow bar. Fix: take «∎» (or «□») from STIX Two Math, and right-align it.
- GD31 — a second fallback: STIXGeneral-Bold on p. 11, the bold «∇» of «a **∇-filtration**» (STIX Two Text Bold has no ∇). Two generations of STIX on one page.
- GD32 — **no page numbers and no running heads on any of the 49 pages**: no word lies outside the text block vertically (logs/pdfcheck2.log; sealed S-14, hit). A referee cannot cite «p. 23, l. 5»; every journal and arXiv preprint carries folios. Fix: a footer with the page number (Chrome's `displayHeaderFooter` with a footer template, or CSS paged-media counters).
