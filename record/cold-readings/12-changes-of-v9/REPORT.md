### B12. §12.3, groups and `ℤ_2`-modules
«The Main Theorem is an isomorphism of groups in Lean and of `ℤ_2`-modules here. The two statements are equivalent, because an additive map between finitely generated `ℤ_2`-modules is `ℤ_2`-linear.» **HOLDS**, in my words: let `f: M → N` be additive, `M, N` finitely generated over `ℤ_2`. `f` is `ℤ`-linear, so `f(2^k M) ⊆ 2^k N`. For `a ∈ ℤ_2` take `a_k ∈ ℤ` with `a − a_k ∈ 2^k ℤ_2`; then `f(ax) − a f(x) = f((a − a_k)x) − (a − a_k) f(x) ∈ 2^k N` for every `k`, and `∩_k 2^k N = 0` because `N ≅ ℤ_2^r ⊕ (finite 2-group)`. So `f` is `ℤ_2`-linear, and its inverse too; a `ℤ_2`-isomorphism is in particular an additive one. Both sides of `M1` are finitely generated `ℤ_2`-modules (`ℤ_2 × Syl_2 K(Q_n)`, the finite `2`-group having a unique `ℤ_2`-structure; `XFam` a finite product of `ℤ_2` and `ℤ_2 ⧸ 2^e`). The pointer «[LC26], §1.3» is right (the Main Theorem row).
