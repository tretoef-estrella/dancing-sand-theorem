### 4.1 What the four sources in `material/sources/` say (read in the text versions; page = printed page)
- **Reiner–Tseng, arXiv:1301.2977v3 (6 Jun 2020), «Critical groups of covering, voltage and signed graphs».** Theorem 1.1 (p. 3, txt l. 159) and **Theorem 1.2 (p. 3, txt l. 188; restated p. 14, l. 854)**: for every signed graph G± parametrizing a double cover G̃ → G there is a short exact sequence 0 → K(G±) → K(G̃) → K(G) → 0, split on p-primary parts for odd p; their worked example (p. 4) shows it does NOT split at p = 2 (0 → Z/2 → Z/4 → Z/2 → 0). Its only cube application (§12.2, p. 32, txt l. 2157; Prop. 12.5, Cor. 12.6) is the projection Q_n → Q_{n−1} with half-loops, recovering Bai's odd-p result. **Verdict: Reiner–Tseng give the surjection/exact sequence that the pencil note §1.2 cites; they do not state the fold law.**
- **Gao–Marx-Kuo–McDonald–Yuen, arXiv:1912.06919v3 (17 Apr 2024).** **Proposition 2.12 (p. 9, txt l. 542):** the F₂-rank of the Laplacian of G(F₂^r, M_r), M_r = (e₁, …, e_r, e₁ + … + e_r) — the folded (r + 1)-cube — is 2^{r−1} − 2^{⌊(r−1)/2⌋}. **Remark 2.13 (p. 10, txt l. 604–607):** this number «also occurs as the number of Z/2Z summands» of K(Q_{r+1}) (Bai, Thm 1.3), and the authors are «not sure if that is a coincident, or a special case of some deeper connection between the elementary divisors of hypercubes and the number of 2-cyclic factors of other Cayley graphs». With n = r + 1 this is exactly the mod-2 layer of the fold law (#unit Smith factors of the folded Laplacian = a_n). **Verdict: they saw the first layer as a coincidence and asked for a «deeper connection»; the fold law (all layers) is such a connection, for the antipodal quotient.** My check 1 adds a negative data point to their question: the non-antipodal quotient Q_n/⟨x₁x₂⟩ does NOT satisfy the doubling law for n = 3..12.
