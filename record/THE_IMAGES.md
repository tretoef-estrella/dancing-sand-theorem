# The author's images: which served, what for, and why not

The author does not write mathematics. When the work was stuck he gave an image in plain words, in Spanish. Each image was translated piece by piece into something that can be counted. The translation was sealed on disk before any measurement. At the end of each flight, the constructor wrote which images served and which did not. This page collects those verdicts.

The list follows the count made by the constructor of flight 9, who was asked how many images had served: **27 distinct images over the nine flights.** Each row was checked against the «which served» section of the flight reports in [flights/](flights/), and against [the paper](../paper/THE_DANCING_SAND_THEOREM_v8.pdf).

**How to read it.**
- **Served** — the translation of the image is the statement or the mechanism of a result: a theorem of the paper, or a lemma of a flight that the paper uses.
- **Served as a frame or a control** — it shaped how a proof was searched for or checked, but no statement has its form.
- **Partly** — one reading served and another did not; both are said.
- **Killed a candidate**, **decorative** — as written.

Results of the paper were read cold by several readers (see [README.md](README.md)). Lemmas named after a flight (for example «Lemma D-even») are that flight's names; the paper absorbed them under its own numbers where it uses them. «Measured» means checked by computation in finitely many cases, not proved.

## Part 2 — the fold law (flights 6 to 9)

| # | Image (the author's words, translated) | Verdict | What it became, or why not | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | **The mirror that doubles**: the cube of dimension `2^k` is the cube of dimension `2^k − 1` seen twice in a mirror, plus one tower on top | Served | Gao et al.'s Conjecture 5.4: each dancer splits into twins with the same clock, and the tower is one dancer. On the Steinberg families, folding the cube is literally doubling the payment. In flight 7 it became Lemma A0, doubling as a second quotient. | flights 6, 7; paper §9.3 (Corollary 1.7) |
| 19 | **The air conditioner and its drain**: «the drops always fall in the same place; the drain of the condensation must be blocked» | Served | The mod-2 layer of the fold law for every `n` (flight 8, Lemma D-even). The water left in the tray falls into the tray one family down, which is full again. It also explained the counterexamples of flight 7: the auditor re-ran them, and they were all houses with a blocked drain. In the paper, its shadow is Lemma 10.18: the dance modulo 2 sees only the operator modulo 2. | flight 8, §5; paper §10.7 |
| 20 | **The U-bar**: «bend a bar into a U, with the same pieces on each side, and hang a weight on each side» | Served | An even family is two odd arms hung on the same bar. «One bend is free, two are not.» In flight 9 it became Lemma U: the glue reaches the bottom of the dance unchanged. In the paper these are Lemmas 10.19 (the glue) and 10.20 (the bend). | flights 8, 9; paper §10.7 |
| 21 | **Dalí's clocks in the sand**: «clocks on the beach are Dalí's; clocks found in the sand belong to whoever lost them» | Served | «The owner pays»: every weight hung on the clock was dropped by a chain of steps, and it pays with the rent of the floors it skips. At the lowest rent the hands do not move. Flight 8 made it Lemma Ω-odd; in the paper it is the mechanism of the rigidity, Theorem 10.12. | flights 8, 9; paper §10.4 |
| 22 | **The cave**: «the deeper you go, do everything by half; the hole narrows; it is the only way down, as in *The Shawshank Redemption*» | Served | The heart of the fold law. It told flight 9 what to measure. The coefficients of every level are functions of twice the position: the ring `𝒦` and the cleanliness Theorem 10.15. The constructor wrote: «Without the measurement "h + 2" I would not have looked for functions of 2y.» | flight 9, §6; paper §10.5 |
| 23 | **The hinges come in pairs** | Served | The two hinges of a door are the two odd arms of an even family. They agree modulo 2 at every level, and that is what makes the glued dance clean. Without the pair the even family would not be danceable. | flight 9, §6; paper §10.7 |
| 24 | **The oiled hinge**: «it is the same: put oil on it, the noise is gone, and the metal shines more» | Partly | «The same» served: the glue is the same at every level, and the whole perturbation lives in the arms («the metal shines more»). The oil itself — row and column operations — was not needed. | flight 9, §6 |
| 25 | **«The bugs are findings»** | Served as a control | Three times in flight 9. The control «generic payments» failed as intended, and showed that the general statement flight 8 had set out (R_gen) is false; this led to `𝒦`. Random units broke at depth, which said the coefficient must be a function of `2y`. A failed association test pointed to the bend. | flight 9, §6 |
| 26 | **Force it to weigh differently**: «force it, as Frobenius was forced to make a wrinkle and could not» | Served as a control | At rent `α = 2` the weights could not be forced to move the hands, not even weights of `2^40`: 616 tries of 616 (the auditor's measurement). That was the signature of a structural law, the rigidity. On lattices without a dance it could be forced, which refuted the auditor's Conjecture B″. | flight 8, §5; paper §10.4 |
| 27 | **The mirror built piece by piece** | Served as a question | It told flight 7 to look for the reflection move by move, and the dance does carry it one move at a time. Its strong reading, «the mirror needs the whole roof», was too strong: the counterexamples lacked one condition modulo 2 (row 19). | flight 7, §5 |

Constructor 9's own estimate was «about 20 served, and at least 9 have the exact shape of a theorem». This page counts 19 that served in full and 6 in part. The difference is one of standard: here, «served» means that the image's translation is the statement or the mechanism of a result in the reports, not only that it has the form of a theorem.

## Not in the count

- **The turned lid**: «a cube open at the top; I put on a lid and turn it». It gave a symmetry: each twist of the lid has a twin, the complementary twist, with exactly the same group. This held in 24 sealed pairs of 24. The fold law is its degenerate end. It is measured, not proved, and was not used: open question 2 of the paper (§11).
- **A screenshot of Jantzen's book** (not an image, but it served): its first line, «Let k be a field throughout this chapter», exposed a wrong citation and led to a proof of the family decomposition that cites no tilting theory.
- **The auditors' own images**: the leaks, the key, the clock with weights, the staircase and the toll. They were given to the author to explain what was missing, not by him. The staircase and the toll became flight 9's Lemma PS; the others were used as vocabulary.
