# Acknowledgements

## To those who asked the question and worked on it before us

This work answers a question that others posed and advanced, and it would not exist without them.

- **Victor Reiner** conjectured in 2001 the number of cyclic factors of the sandpile group of the cube and the number of its factors `ℤ/2`, kept the question of its 2-part alive, and, with **Dennis Tseng**, gave the exact sequence of a double cover that frames the fold law.
- **Hua Bai** proved those two conjectures, determined every odd part, and published, from Reiner's computations, the table of the 2-parts for `n ≤ 11`, which later work reproduced and with which our rule agrees.
- **Joshua Ducey and Deelan Jalil**, and **David Chandler, Peter Sin and Qing Xiang**, stated the problem precisely. The monomial basis in which our Laplacian is written is the one of Chandler, Sin and Xiang.
- **Jiyang Gao, Jared Marx-Kuo, Vaughan McDonald and Chi Ho Yuen** determined the largest cyclic factors, posed the conjectures that are proved here, and wrote the remark on a coincidence («a special case of some deeper connection») that the fold law explains.
- **Michael Larsen**, **Stephen Donkin**, and **Daniel Tubbenhauer and Paul Wedrich** described the tilting modules of `SL_2` in characteristic `2` and their multiplicities, which our recursion reproduces.

The full list of the works used, and how each was used, is in §1.8 and the references of the paper; the literature search, with the list of the sources read in the original, is in [record/literature/](record/literature/LITERATURE_SWEEP.md).

## To the AI system

**Claude (Anthropic).** The mathematics of this repository was developed by instances of Claude, Anthropic's AI system, working under the author's direction in separate roles:
- constructors, who found and wrote the proofs (the nine flights);
- auditors, who re-derived every step with their own code, wrote the missions, and wrote the paper;
- cold readers, who read the chains and the text without access to the audits.

The instances took names («Grepy el volador», «Grepy Bross», «Grepy Muchas Pilas», «Grepy Escribano», and others); their work is in [record/](record/README.md), and their story in [THE_STORY_AND_THE_NUMBERS.md](THE_STORY_AND_THE_NUMBERS.md).

## The limits of this

**No human expert has yet refereed this work, and no proof assistant has checked it.** Every reading so far was made by an instance of the same AI system. The readers worked separately, never saw each other's code, and did not see the audits, but they are not independent in the way that different human experts are. The paper says so (§13).

The author, **Rafael Amichis Luengo**, directed the project and takes responsibility for its content.
